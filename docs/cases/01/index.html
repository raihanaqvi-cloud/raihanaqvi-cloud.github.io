THE FINDING

THE PROBLEM WASN’T SIMPLY THE NUMBER OF FIELDS.

The form never told operators where they were, what was missing, or what happened when they saved. The complexity lived in the product model, and the interface didn’t help anyone through it.

THE FIX

SIX MOVES.

01
SECTION NAVIGATION
ANSWERS CLUE 01

THE OUTCOME

THE FORM STOPPED MAKING USERS HUNT.

Save clicks per product went from about 4, one per section, to 1 at most, with autosave doing the rest. Follow-up Hotjar recordings showed scrolling visibly reduced. In daily use.

THE CONSTRAINT

no two forms alike

Every customer defines their own attributes, so the form is generated, not drawn. Any fix had to work for forms we would never see.

WHAT I’D DO DIFFERENTLY
and now we do

Bring the front-end developer in sooner. We designed section reordering before anyone had sized the dev effort, and it was parked. Now our front-end lead joins the first stakeholder reviews.
