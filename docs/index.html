THE METHOD

  1. 01
    Find the real problem

    What people report is rarely the real problem. I trace it back to the source.

  2. 02
    Understand the system

    The data and rules behind a product shape what the screen can do.

  3. 03
    Listen to everyone

    Support, engineering, sales and users each know part of the answer.

  4. 04
    Ship in small steps

    A redesign that can’t be built step by step won’t get built.
